Apple Products Store for Mozambique Site Plan

WDD 231 Individual Website Project

Site Name

Apple Products Store for Mozambique

This name was selected because the website will focus on helping customers in Mozambique learn about Apple products, compare options, and find trusted buying information.

Optional domain: applemozstore.com

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide a simple information site for people in Mozambique who are interested in Apple products. The site will provide product categories, product details, prices in Mozambican Metical, customer reviews, and trusted buying information.

The website will help visitors compare products such as iPhones, iPads, MacBooks, Apple Watches, and Apple TV before making a purchase decision.

Scenarios

  • Which iPhone model is best for my budget in Mozambique?
  • Where can I find trusted information about Apple product prices in Metical?
  • How can I contact the store or learn about trusted partner sellers?

Color Scheme

The color scheme uses dark navy for strong branding, blue for calls to action, and light colors for a clean technology-style background.

Dark Navy

#0f172a

Used for the header, footer, main headings, and strong branding areas.

Apple Blue

#2563eb

Used for buttons, links, borders, highlights, and call-to-action elements.

Light Background

#f8fafc

Used for the page background and clean content areas.

Typography

Poppins will be used for headings, titles, and important section labels. It gives the website a modern technology feel.

Roboto will be used for body text, paragraphs, lists, and general page content. It is clean, readable, and works well for product information.
