About Me

My name is Osvaldo João, and I am originally from Mozambique. I am currently studying web development and working toward a career in technology. I enjoy learning how to create clean, responsive, accessible, and user-friendly websites that work well on computers, tablets, and mobile devices. My goal is to continue strengthening my programming and software development skills so that I can build useful digital products. In the future, I hope to use technology to create opportunities, support businesses, and help solve problems in communities in Mozambique and around the world. I believe technology can connect people, improve access to information, and make everyday life easier.
